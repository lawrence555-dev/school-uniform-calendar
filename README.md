# School Calendar & Daily Uniform Notification System (2026 Term 1)
### 學校行事曆與每日著裝穿搭通知系統 ✕ ปฏิทินโรงเรียนและการแต่งกายประจำวัน

> 📱 **Mobile-First Progressive Web Application**
> Real-time daily uniform notifications (House Shirt, School Uniform, PE Kit, Swimming gear), Day 1 – Day 8 rotation cycle, and school events with multi-language support (**Traditional Chinese, English, Thai**).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Prominent "Today's Dress Code" Notification (今日著裝醒目通知)**:
   - Defaults to **2026-10-01 (Thu) - Day 7 ➔ House Shirt (學院服)**.
   - Live visual icon, full uniform details (tops, bottoms, footwear), and special school event banners.
   - **Tomorrow's Preview**: Proactive packing alerts (e.g., reminding parents to pack swimsuits, caps, goggles, and towels the night before).
2. **Interactive Calendar Grid & Timeline List (月曆與清單雙模式)**:
   - **October 2026**: Quarter 1 end, Fall Break (10/12–10/16), King Bhumibol Memorial Holiday (10/19), Quarter 2 start, Progress Reports, Halloween celebrations.
   - **November 2026**: Three-Way Conferences (11/05 & 11/06), Faculty PD Day (11/09), Food Fiesta & Thanksgiving (11/20), Loy Krathong (11/25).
   - **December 2026**: National Father's Day (12/04), King Bhumibol Birthday Holiday (12/07), ASA Ends (12/11), Christmas Celebrations & 12:00 Early Dismissal / Semester 1 End (12/18), Winter Break.
3. **Tri-lingual Real-Time Switcher (英 / 泰 / 繁中 三語言即時切換)**:
   - 繁體中文 (Traditional Chinese)
   - English (EN)
   - ภาษาไทย (Thai)
4. **Smart Category Filters (分類快速篩選)**:
   - All (全部)
   - House Shirt (學院服)
   - School Uniform (校服)
   - PE Kit (體育服)
   - Swimming Session (需帶游泳)
   - School Holidays (放假不上課)
   - Special Events (重要活動)
5. **Interactive Uniform Guide Modal (制服穿搭圖文指南)**:
   - Detailed packing and dress guidelines for House Shirt, Regular Uniform, PE Kit, and Swimming Equipment.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework**: React 19 + Vite 5
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS (Dark Slate aesthetic with vibrant color coding)
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Fonts**: Google Fonts (Work Sans, Noto Sans Thai, Noto Sans TC, JetBrains Mono)
